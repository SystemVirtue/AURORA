# AURORA — 60-SECOND EXECUTIVE PITCH

## AURORA — Dawn for Transparent AI

### 0–10s — The hook

Today's AI is a black box. It gives answers, but you cannot see how it decided, what it believed a moment ago, or which source actually caused the output. That is not accountable intelligence; it is a transaction.

### 11–25s — The problem

Most systems treat each LLM call as isolated inference. They conflate conversation history with memory, model assertions with facts, and explanation text with provenance. They lack durable state, temporal awareness and a real audit trail. When something goes wrong, you cannot reliably rewind, investigate or correct the record.

### 26–45s — AURORA

AURORA changes that. She is a persistent, event-sourced cognitive substrate where beliefs, evidence and decisions are durable, replayable and inspectable. She separates facts from beliefs, tracks what is unknown, preserves disagreement and records how conclusions were reached. QUORUM adds collective reasoning when multiple models measurably improve the result.

### 46–60s — The close

AURORA is model-agnostic and designed to outlast any single vendor. She integrates with frontier and open models while preserving a canonical cognitive history. She does not just generate answers — she makes machine cognition accountable.

**AURORA: Bringing light to the AI black box.**
